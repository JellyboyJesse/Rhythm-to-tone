Below about 10 hits a second you hear separate hits. Above about 20 loops a second the whole loop fuses into one pitch at the loop rate, and the pattern becomes its timbre. Snap to notes rounds the speed to the nearest semitone once it is a pitch.

03Rings

Each ring spreads its hits as evenly as possible over its steps (a Euclidean rhythm). A ring with equal steps and hits is a plain pulse. An LFO on Rotate turns the ring smoothly, between steps.

06Library

Too many overlapping sounds: some were dropped. Shorten the click or the sample slice, or slow down.

Click pitch sets a formant. It stays fixed in Hz while the loop pitch moves, like a vowel. Each click keeps the pitch and length it had when it started, so LFOs on the click change it hit by hit. Detune gives each ring a slightly different speed, so the tone splits into separate beating voices. Shared vibrato wobbles every ring together, which helps the ear hear one sound.

Patch scale
Slot

The patch scale is the one every sequence uses unless it chooses its own. A slot holds a sequence that several dots can share: editing it from any of them changes them all, while each dot keeps its own place in it. Clearing a slot gives each dot that used it a copy of its own.

Slot

Draw in the box to make a shape. It then shows as a numbered button in every LFO's row of shapes.

Load or drop an audio file on a slot. Then set a dot to Sample.

A sample dot plays a slice of the recording each time it fires. Pitch changes the playback speed, Start and Slice choose the part, and Length scales the slice. Short slices get soft edges, so at tone speeds a sample works as a grain. A new hit on a slot cuts the one before it unless the slot is set to Overlap. Each slot's play mode, envelope, filter and modulators are set under Playback in the sample window. Samples are kept in this browser, not in the patch.

An instrument is a full voice: a source, an envelope, a filter and a way of playing. It is edited in its own window and saved in a slot here. A dot set to Instrument plays it as a note: Pitch is the note, Level the velocity, Length how long it is held.

The top wheel unrolled: one lane per ring, left to right in time. A tick is a click, a bar is a sample or a note for as long as it sounds, and a bracket is a wheel inside a dot with its own sounds drawn small. The curves underneath are the LFOs over the same stretch of time. Pause (K) freezes it so you can read it. Click a sound to select its dot, and drag up or down to change its level.

08Sections

Sections
Trips1

A section is the whole wheel saved under a name: its rings and the wheels inside them, speed, click settings, LFOs and sequencer steps. The wheel on screen is always the selected section, so editing the wheel edits that section. Trips is how many times round it lasts. Send to the arranger renders the section as audio, one clip per ring, and puts the clips at the end of the arrangement. They are arranged, cut and mixed in the arranger window. All sections share one Library of samples, instruments and LFO shapes.

10Circles

16

Circle n spins n times per loop. Its size and starting angle come from the pattern's nth Fourier coefficient. Chained together, the circles draw the pattern: each spike is a hit. More circles give sharper hits.
